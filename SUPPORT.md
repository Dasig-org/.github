# Getting help

- **A question, or an idea you're not sure about yet:** start a thread in
  [Dasig-org Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/Dasig-org/discussions).
- **Something is broken, or a clear piece of work:** open an **Issue** using one of the templates.
- **A security or privacy problem:** don't open an issue. Follow the repository's `SECURITY.md`
  (the **Security** tab → "Report a vulnerability", or email hello@dasig.org with "Security" in the subject).
- **Anything else** (partnerships, a group wanting to pilot Dasig, press): hello@dasig.org.

This is a small team. We read everything, usually within a week, but we can't always reply quickly.
